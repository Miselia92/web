description: |-
  B&W Film,36 works,Dimensions and materials variable according to exhibition site

  Project: Blank Film is a more intimate body of work. It begins with the sudden death of a close friend and a roll of film he left behind—exposed, yet entirely undeveloped. Four years after the incident, the ongoing process of making and exhibiting becomes a way to respond to and reflect upon death, memory, and emotion: memories that are at times continuous, at times piercingly vivid, at times suspended in a haze. Through this project, I also question time itself—what is lost after death, and what, if anything, remains.
  Before my friend passed away, he left behind a roll of film he had already finished shooting. When I developed it, the roll came out entirely blank—exposed, yet containing nothing. It felt like his absence in my life: present only as a void, existing through emptiness.
  Only later did I realize that the thirty-six nearly blank frames—marked only by scratches and specks of dust—resembled fragments of a night sky. Thirty-six different constellations. And within them, perhaps, everything was already there.
description_zh: |-
  黑白底片，36件，視場地調整輸出大小、材質

  《白片計畫》為較具私密性的創作計畫，內容以年輕好友的突然死亡與其所留下的一捲「已拍過卻毫無顯影」的底片出發，在事件發生後四年的現在，透過進行中的創作與展覽計劃去回應與探討死亡、記憶與情感，那種時而連續、時而深刻、時而恍惚的記憶，同時探問著時間以及死亡後所失去與所留下的。
  朋友離世前留下了一卷已拍攝完的底片，我沖洗後是卷白片，拍完了卻什麼也沒拍到，就如同他在我生命中的缺席狀態，以留白的方式存在著。我後來才發現，這36張幾乎空白、只剩下刮痕與塵點的底片卻像是一片片星空，36面不同的星空，其實什麼都在其中了。
