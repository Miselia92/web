  陳宇弘 (1999, 台灣)

  畢業於國立政治大學廣播電視學系，現就讀於國立臺北藝術大學藝術跨域研究所。
  創作以影像為基礎，創作範疇涵蓋靜態攝影、錄像、版畫、紀錄片、實驗電影、動畫與劇情片等。

Chun Yu Hong (b.1999,Taiwan)

Graduated from the Department of Radio and Television at National Chengchi University, currently pursuing a master’s degree at the Graduate Institute for Transdisciplinary Arts at Taipei National University of the Arts.
An image-based artist whose practice spans still photography, printmaking, documentaries, experimental films, animation, and narrative films.
His work primarily explores themes of time, space, memory, and postcolonialism, approaching images as a means of social engagement, communication, and emotional expression.
